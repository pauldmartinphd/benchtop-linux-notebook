## Power at the disk prompt

- A laptop that restarts in a bag and stops at the disk prompt stays on until its battery is empty: logind and GNOME, which suspend it, start only after the disk is unlocked.
- After a restart that unlocks, the sources say the login screen suspends: logind once its 30-second startup grace period ends with the lid closed (unless an external monitor or dock is connected), GNOME after 15 minutes idle with the lid open. Not tested.
- After a crash, the kernel can only restart or hang (its panic code has no power-off); kdump could run a script after a crash, but needs the encrypted disk to save the dump and covers kernel crashes only. The direction taken: a power-off in the initrd while the disk is locked.
