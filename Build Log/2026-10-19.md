## kmscon

- kmscon (the maintained revival, 10.0.4) is packaged in openSUSE:Factory with the freetype renderer and libseat.
- It replaces fbcon on the virtual terminals, and TCBL has none: NAutoVTs=0, ReserveVT=0, getty@ disabled, tty1 owned by GDM. It starts late, so kernel messages and panics stay on fbcon, and it does not host the emergency sulogin shell.
- Open questions if adopted: reopening a text-login surface, SELinux denials (the openSUSE package carries a patch for missing policy), and the DRM-master handoff with GDM.
- Decision: captured as an idea in Boot and Init, not adopted. Committed October 21 in `797ee82`.
