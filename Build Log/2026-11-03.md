## The laptop restarted and waited at the disk prompt

- On November 2 at 22:08:26 the laptop (silversurfer) entered suspend (s2idle); the next kernel started at 22:09:56, and the laptop waited at the disk prompt until the key was typed on November 3 at 14:57.
- The automatic update was not the cause: transactional-update last ran on November 1 at 21:26, the timer was skipped on November 2 at 21:15 (on battery, `ConditionACPower`), `REBOOT_METHOD=notify` was in effect, and rebootmgr is not installed. The automatic update of November 1 at 20:47 had failed, probably on the YaST conflict.
- The AMD reset reason was "software wrote 0xE to reset control register 0xCF9" during an ACPI power state transition: a reset requested by software, either the kernel after a crash or the firmware. `kernel.panic` was 5; no crash record in pstore. An HP firmware ACPI error (`EC0._Q0A`, `AE_NOT_FOUND`) was logged two seconds before the suspend. Cause not known.
- The disk did not unlock because PCR 7 no longer matched the TPM policy; the boot of 21:33 on November 2 had already needed the key. After the unlock, sdbootutil's prediction update failed: no recovery PIN was reachable.
- The clock was 5 hours ahead from chronyd's start until its first synchronization, on every boot: openSUSE's default `-s` (since September 14) has chronyd set the clock from the hardware clock, which chrony reads as local time unless configured otherwise, while the hardware clock keeps UTC. The offset was first guessed from `journalctl --list-boots`, withdrawn when the hardware clock proved to be in UTC, and confirmed in the timestamps after the unlock.
