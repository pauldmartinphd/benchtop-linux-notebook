## Pattern split

- Optional packages split out of the base pattern: admin (remote administration and servers: Samba and NFS servers, Cockpit, database clients, Ansible, OpenTofu, Helm, kubectl) and languages (programming languages). Tools for debugging a running system, such as Wireshark and tcpdump, stay in base.
- The sets are chosen in the installer: a tik module asks during installation and installs the chosen sets into the new system.
- Image Build31.1 built.

## USB device emulation

- Question: integrating raw-gadget.
- Correction: Tumbleweed has no kernel-default-extra package. kernel-default carries all built modules; the "-" in supported.conf is a support-status flag, not a packaging split. raw_gadget.ko and the rest of the gadget stack (g_hid, g_ether, g_mass_storage, the usb_f_* functions, gadgetfs, libcomposite) are in Build31.1.
- dummy_hcd, the virtual USB device controller, is not built by openSUSE. Custom kernel item.
- Device controllers in the image: dwc3, musb_hdrc, pch_udc. Only dwc3 is relevant on x86, and only where the firmware exposes it.
