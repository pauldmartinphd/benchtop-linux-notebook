## JACK through PipeWire

- Checked against Build31.1: PipeWire 1.6.9 is the JACK server; there is no standalone jackd. pipewire-jack and pipewire-libjack-0_3 provide libjack. /etc/ld.so.conf.d/pipewire-jack-x86_64.conf puts PipeWire's libjack on the linker path, so JACK applications connect with no setup; pw-jack is also installed. rtkit and the rtprio and memlock limits provide realtime scheduling. Flatpak applications use their runtime's own PipeWire JACK layer.
