## Findings: flashrom

- The internal programmer (the board's own flash) is blocked on the stock kernel by two things. Secure Boot lockdown closes /dev/mem, /dev/port and iopl. The spi-intel driver binds the PCH flash controller, and with CONFIG_IO_STRICT_DEVMEM=y its registers are closed to /dev/mem.
- CONFIG_STRICT_DEVMEM is not the problem; iomem=relaxed is not needed.
- External USB programmers (CH341A, FT2232, Dediprog, serprog) need only a udev rule. The programmer VID:PIDs in the note are marked for verification.
